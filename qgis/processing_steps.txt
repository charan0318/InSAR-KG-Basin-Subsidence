QGIS Processing Workflow – Subsidence Analysis

---

1. Import Raster

* Load velocity raster: velocity_masked_mm.tif

---

2. Visualization

* Renderer: Singleband pseudocolor
* Color ramp: Blue–White–Red
* Range:

  * Minimum: -20 mm/year
  * Maximum: +20 mm/year

---

3. Filtering (Noise Reduction)

Raster Calculator expression:

("velocity_clean@1" > -20 AND "velocity_clean@1" < 0) * "velocity_clean@1" / (("velocity_clean@1" > -20 AND "velocity_clean@1" < 0))

Output:
velocity_filtered.tif

---

4. Threshold Extraction (High Subsidence)

Raster Calculator expression:

"velocity_filtered@1" < -10

Output:
subsidence_mask.tif

---

5. Noise Removal (Sieve)

Tool: Raster → Sieve

Parameters:

* Threshold: 200–500 pixels
* Connectivity: 8

---

6. Raster to Polygon Conversion

Tool:
Raster → Conversion → Polygonize

Input:
subsidence_mask.tif

Output:
subsidence_zones.shp

---

7. Remove Small Polygons

* Open attribute table
* Delete polygons with small area (noise)

---

8. Dissolve Polygons

Tool:
Vector → Geoprocessing → Dissolve

* Merge all connected polygons

Output:
final_subsidence_zones.shp

---

9. Zonal Statistics

Tool:
Raster → Zonal Statistics

Input raster:
velocity_filtered.tif

Zone layer:
final_subsidence_zones.shp

Outputs:

* Mean subsidence
* Minimum subsidence
* Maximum subsidence

---

10. Final Map Preparation

* Overlay polygons on velocity raster
* Add:

  * Legend
  * Scale bar
  * North arrow
  * Title

---

END OF WORKFLOW
